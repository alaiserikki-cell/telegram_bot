Something that frustrated me recently — and I want to write about it carefully because it's a sourcing issue. We were reviewing a potential new emollient ingredient from a supplier. The spec sheet listed it as cold-pressed. Cold-pressing is a temperature-controlled extraction process, below 49 degrees Celsius typically, that preserves fatty acid profiles and doesn't degrade heat-sensitive compounds. When I asked for the processing documentation, they eventually sent over a production log that showed temperatures between 70 and 85 degrees Celsius during extraction. That's not cold-pressing. That's standard heat processing. The cold-pressed claim on the spec sheet was either a labelling error or not. I don't know which. We didn't use the ingredient. But we would have, if I hadn't asked for the documentation.
